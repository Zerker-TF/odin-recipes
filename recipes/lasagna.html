Lasagna

Description

This lasagna recipe takes a little work, but its worth it!

How long does it take?

      Prep Time: 30 mins
      Cook Time: 2hrs 30 mins
      Total time: 3hrs 15 mins

Ingredients

  • 1kg of ground beef
  • 2 minced onion
  • 500gr of cheese of choice
  • 1/2 cup of meat broth
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1/2 cup of water
  • 8 lasagna noodles
  • tomato sauce (or sauce of choice)

Step by Step

  • Cook the ground beef with the minced onion and garlic until the meat is almost done
  • Add some meat brooth and let it simmer for 5 minutes
  • Meanwhile, add the lasagna noodles in boiling water until they soften
  • Pre heat the oven at 200 C
  • In an oven capable pot, start building the lasagna
  • the order is: sauce -> noodle -> meat -> noodle -> cheese -> sauce
  • When you add the final layer, cover with grated cheese
  • cook at low heat for 2hrs
