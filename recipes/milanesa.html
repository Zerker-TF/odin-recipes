Milanesa

Description

Tasty meal perfect for lunch!

How long does it take?

  • Prep Time: 20 minutes
  • Cook Time: 30 minutes
  • Total Time: 50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1kg of Bola de lomo / 1kg of chicken breast
  • 2 eggs
  • 200gr of breadcrumbs
  • Salt,Pepper,Paprika,Oregano

Step by Step

  • Cut the meat/chicken into layers
  • Bate the eggs and add the condiments
  • Drip the meat/chicken into the egg batter
  • Pass it over the breadcrumbs and cover it whole
  • pre heat oven at 200 C
  • Once all the milanesas are done, into the oven for 20 minutes then turn them over for another 10 minutes
