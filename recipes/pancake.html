Pancake

Description

Quick, easy and tasty snack for every morning!

How long does it take?

  • Prep Time: 5 minutes
  • Cook Time: 1 minute per pancake

Ingredients

  • 300gr of 0000 Flour
  • 2 Eggs
  • 20g of Melted Butter
  • 30ml of Milk
  • Sugar and Salt to taste

Step by Step

  • In a bowl, mix the flour, sugar and salt
  • Add the two batted eggs to the dry mix
  • while still mixing, add the melted butter
  • While still mixing, start adding the milk, little by litte
  • Once the mixture has a nice soft texture, heat up a pan on low heat with a little butter
  • Cover the surface with the mixture and turn it around after 20 seconds
  • Repeat after no more mixture in the bowl
